SPARTA

Hold the line. Burn Athens.

📱 Play fullscreen: tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Spartan RTS from your home screen (portrait or landscape).

Goal

Destroy the Athenian Agora on the far side of the map. Lose yours and Sparta falls. Athenian attack waves grow over time.

Economy

Helots gather 🌾 food (olive groves, farms), 🪵 wood (trees) and 🔶 bronze (rock mines), then haul it home on their own.

Touch

• Tap a unit/building to select it — taps near a unit count, no need to be exact
• Tap ground/enemy/resource to command
• Drag one finger to pan · two fingers to pan/pinch-zoom
• Press & hold (green ring fills), then drag to box-select — drag near the edge to scroll while selecting
• Army selects all soldiers · double-tap a unit or building to select all of that type (e.g. every Barracks; training goes to the shortest queue)
• Tap minimap to jump

PC (mouse + keys)

• Left click select · drag a box · Shift+click / Shift+drag adds · double-click a unit = all of that type on screen
• Right click = move / attack / gather (whatever you click on) · right-click the minimap to send units there
• Wheel zooms toward the cursor · middle- or right-drag, WASD / arrows, or mouse at the screen edge pans · click the minimap to jump · Space = home
• Ctrl+1–9 save a group (Alt+1–9 if the browser takes Ctrl), Shift+1–9 add to it, 1–9 select it, press twice to jump to it
• Hotkeys are on the buttons (Q E C H Y T B R O F L N V P K G M Z X I) · Shift+A army · Esc cancel

Counters: Hoplites (spears) beat Cavalry · Cavalry beat Archers & Helots — gallop in for a double-damage charge · Archers beat Hoplites, unless they are in Phalanx. Archers barely scratch buildings; Hoplites wreck them. Upgrades: research at the Agora, Barracks, Archery Range and Forge (O).
Phalanx (P): hoplites standing close together take far less damage, but march slower. Stance (G): ⚔ Aggressive soldiers engage nearby enemies on their own; 🛡 Hold soldiers only follow your orders. Attacks you order are always followed through. Atk-Move (M): march and fight along the way. ⭐ Heroes: train Leonidas at the Agora (🌾200 🔶100). He is a tough spearman whose aura makes nearby hoplites hit harder and take less damage. Select him with the ⭐ button or Y, then tap the gold ⚡ This Is Sparta! button (T) to kick and stun the enemies in front of him. He levels up from kills (max ★5), heals when out of combat, and returns to the Agora 50s after falling. Athens has Pericles, an archer-statesman whose speech heals and inspires his troops.
